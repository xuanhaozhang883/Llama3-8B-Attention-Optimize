CATS-R4 A frontend evidence limitations, 2026-09-13

1. Frontend E2E uses HEAD_DIM=4 and deterministic BF16=1.0 Q/K service models
   to keep the complete 4096-row simulation practical in Icarus. The separate
   HEAD_DIM=128 scheduler regression verifies 33,816,576 valid QK MACs.
2. The current environment has no discoverable Vivado/XSim tools. No current
   frontend real-Xilinx-IP XSim, OOC synthesis, PPA, WNS/TNS, or CDC report is
   claimed. This is the remaining A READY TO MERGE gate.
3. B4 numeric RTL, production weight/V, system CDC/DMA/output, board top,
   BIT/XSA/ELF, and board measurements are C-owned and outside this artifact.
4. Icarus reports its known constant-select sensitivity warnings for several
   always_comb/always_ff blocks; compile exits and every retained regression
   PASS marker are checked by the runners.
